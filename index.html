How it works

    
The song is split into thousands of overlapping slices, about 43 milliseconds each, and each slice into a thousand frequency bands. Two passes then adjust the level of each band in each slice, and the slices are stitched back together with their original timing, so nothing about the attack or the stereo image moves.

    
The static pass follows the adaptive spectral denoise method shared on r/SunoAI: learn what the hiss sounds like from the quietest moments, subtract it gently in the bass and more firmly up top. The shimmer pass is new. It looks for sound that is both brief and narrow, the signature of that glassy grain, and pulls it down to the level of its surroundings. A drum hit rises across every band at once and a held note lasts, so both are left as they are.

    
MP3 works, but the MP3 encoder has already thrown away detail and added its own artifacts, so start from the WAV download when you can. The cleaned file always comes out as WAV.
